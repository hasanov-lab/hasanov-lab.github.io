ISM-RCC

Immunotherapy Survival Model - Renal Cell Carcinoma

ISM-RCC is a machine learning-based tool developed by Hasanov Lab to estimate overall survival for patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma receiving first line therapy ipilimumab and nivolumab.

It depends on selected clinical and laboratory features to estimate personalized risk assessment for the patient.
